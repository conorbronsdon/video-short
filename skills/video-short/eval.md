# Video Short checks

Answer each check yes or no before sharing the render. Fix any no first.

## Cut

1. Did you read every line of the raw transcript, not only the obvious repeats?
2. Does the clean transcript keep only the last complete take of each repeated line?
3. Does every kept word sound whole, with no clipped first or last syllable?
4. Does speech start within about 0.1 seconds of the first frame?

## Plan

5. Does the hook land in the first 3 seconds?
6. Is the speaker on screen for at least half the Short, with no full-screen B-roll shot longer than about 3.5 seconds?
7. Does each visual show something the words don't already say?
8. Does every non-speaker shot move: real footage, a card building row by row, a counting number, or an animated graphic, with no static slide?
9. Did the user review a draft on the review page, and were all of their notes applied to `edit.md`?

## Render

10. Does `qa.py` pass every check?
11. In the QA frames, do captions stay clear of the mouth and chin, and are clips and cards readable at phone size?
12. Does each emphasis box mark a name, number, or main claim, with no more than about one every 10-15 seconds?
13. Are sound effects off unless the user asked for them, and quiet under the voice when on?
