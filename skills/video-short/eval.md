# Video Short checks

Answer each check yes or no before sharing the final render. Fix any no first.

## Cut

1. Did you read every line of the raw transcript, not only the obvious repeats?
2. Does the clean transcript keep only the last complete take of each repeated line?
3. Does every kept word sound whole, with no clipped first or last syllable?

## Plan

4. Does the hook land in the first 3 seconds?
5. Is the speaker on screen for at least half the Short, with no full-screen B-roll shot longer than about 3.5 seconds?
6. Does each visual show something the words don't already say?
7. Does every off-camera shot move, with no static slide?
8. Did the user approve the plan in chat, and was every generated asset one they approved?

## Review and final

9. Was every note from the review page applied to `edit.md`, and did the user approve the last draft?
10. Does `qa.py` pass every check?
11. In the QA frames, do captions stay clear of the mouth and chin, and are clips and cards readable at phone size?
12. Does each emphasis box mark a name, number, or main claim, no more than about once every 10-15 seconds?
